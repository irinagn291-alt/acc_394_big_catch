Start at Willow Lake in Big Catch, an offline, single-player fishing game for iPhone and iPad. Cast, watch for a bite, and set the hook. Reel gently and ease off as needed to manage line tension. Every fish you land is weighed, logged, and released.

Explore four illustrated lakes and discover 12 species. Gain experience to unlock new fishing spots and spend coins earned in the game on tackle. Upgraded rods soften the rise in line tension, while upgraded lures improve your odds of hooking rarer fish.

See personal bests and recent catches in the Trophy Room. Meet monthly goals for catch count, species variety, and total weight to earn keepsake badges.

Choose metric or imperial weights, adjust haptics, or turn on Calm water to reduce scenery animation and make faster reeling progress. Set optional reminders for bite hours and the start of the month, or check bite hours at a glance with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Larger Home Screen widgets also show progress.
