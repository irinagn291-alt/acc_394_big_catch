# Big Catch

## What it is

Big Catch is an offline catch-and-release fishing game for players who want short fishing sessions and a collection to build over time. Players cast, react to bites, manage line tension, and earn coins and exploration XP to upgrade tackle and unlock four lakes with twelve fish species.

## Launch and onboarding

1. The system launch screen has no app copy or controls. The app then opens directly to “Fish,” with “BIG CATCH” and “A quiet cast” at the top. There is no welcome screen, tutorial sequence, or permission prompt on launch.
2. On first launch, the header shows 80 coins. The lake scene shows “OFFLINE LAKE,” “Willow Lake,” either “Lively water” or “Gentle water” according to the device clock, and “Find your rhythm. Let the water lead.”
3. The first action card reads “Ready when you are” and “Cast into the lake, then watch for a bite. Every fish returns to the water.” It shows “Willow rod · Copper spoon.” Tap “Cast your line” to begin. The instructions change as the cast progresses.
4. The screen also shows “Daily bite window” and “Game conditions follow your device clock.” The four tabs and the gear control labeled “Settings” for accessibility are available immediately. On a returning cold launch, “Fish” opens with the saved lake and gear, and “Your waterside supplies” may appear if enough time has elapsed.

## Screens

The shared header shows “BIG CATCH,” the current screen title, the coin balance, and the gear button with the accessibility label “Settings.” The balance has the accessibility label “Coins” followed by the amount. The bottom buttons “Fish,” “Trophies,” “Tackle,” and “Lakes” open their respective screens.

### “Fish” — “A quiet cast”

The lake illustration shows the current lake, its water status, and a float during the wait and bite. The “Daily bite window” card shows 6:00–9:00 and 17:00–20:00 in the device’s time format. The current rod and lure appear on the action card except while reeling.

| State | What appears and what to tap |
|---|---|
| Ready | “Ready when you are.” The card reads “Cast into the lake, then watch for a bite. Every fish returns to the water.” Tap “Cast your line” to start. |
| Waiting | “Waiting for a ripple.” The card reads “A little patience. The float will move when a fish is ready.” A spinner shows while waiting. “Bring the line in” cancels the cast and returns to the ready state. |
| Bite | The lake displays “A bite!” The card reads “Time to set the hook” and “Tap to hook the fish before it swims away.” Tap “Set the hook” to start reeling. |
| Reeling | “Keep a gentle rhythm,” a “Line tension” percentage and meter, and a separate catch progress bar. The instructions read “Reel to raise tension. Ease to lower it. Keep tension between 22% and 82%.” Tap “Reel gently” for a short burst of reeling. Tap “Ease” to stop the burst and let tension fall. |
| Catch | “A moment to remember,” the species, its weight, and “Recorded in your trophy room. Released with care.” The catch is saved and released automatically. “Cast again” starts another cast. |
| Miss | “Another quiet chance” with either “The fish swam away. Cast again.” or “The line went loose or too tight. Try a gentler rhythm.” “Cast again” starts another cast. |

When idle coins are awarded, “Your waterside supplies” shows “Coins gathered while you were away: 〈amount〉.” The close icon, labeled “Dismiss” for accessibility, hides the notice. The coins are already in the balance.

### “Trophies” — “Trophy room”

- The summary counters are “Species,” “Lakes,” and “Catches.” They show discovered species out of 12, unlocked lakes out of 4, and total landed catches.
- “Your collection” reads “Every catch is recorded here. Explore new lakes to find all twelve species.” Each card shows a fish name and its lake. Undiscovered fish show “Not discovered yet.” Discovered fish show “Best” followed by the best weight and “Caught” followed by the count. The cards have no tap action.
- “Season on the water” shows the current month and “Monthly goals award keepsake badges only. New goals begin each calendar month.” The three goals are “Catch twelve fish,” “Discover six species,” and “Record 30 kg this month.” Each has a progress bar and a count. “Collected badges” shows the cumulative total. The six-species goal counts distinct species caught that month, including species already in the collection.
- “Recent catches” appears after the first catch and displays up to ten entries, newest first, with species, date and weight. The rows have no tap action.

Either button clears an active cast if it is waiting for a bite or a hook. During reeling, the button still opens “Fish,” but the current lake and attempt are kept.

### “Settings”

Tap the gear from any tab to open “Settings.” Tap “Done” to close it and return to the tab.

- “Make yourself comfortable” contains “Metric weights,” “Gentle haptics,” and “Calm water.” “Metric weights” switches catch weights between kg and lb. “Gentle haptics” controls feedback for bites, landed catches, and tackle changes. “Calm water” reduces lake motion and speeds up reeling progress. The explanation reads “Calm water reduces motion and shortens reeling time.” Metric weights and haptics start on. Calm water starts off.
- “Local reminders” reads “Choose daily bite-window reminders and a monthly season note. Notifications are optional and stay on this device.” “Remind me” starts off. Turning it on requests notification permission if needed and schedules both kinds of reminders. Turning it off cancels pending reminders. Status text can be “Not requested,” “Allowed,” “Disabled in system settings,” or “Could not schedule reminders. Try again.” In the denied state, “Open system settings” opens the app’s device settings.
- “Widgets” contains “Preview widgets,” which opens the “Widgets” screen. The section reads “Add Big Catch widgets from your Home Screen or Lock Screen. They show local bite hours, lake progress and your collection without a connection.”
- “Your data” reads “No accounts, analytics, tracking, purchases or advertising. Your collection stays on this device and its widget group. Deleting the app removes local progress; system backups follow your device settings.” It also reads “Idle supplies accrue every five minutes, up to eight hours away. Fish are only recorded after you reel them in.”
- “Erase local progress” opens the confirmation “Erase your collection, tackle and coins?” with the message “This cannot be undone.” Confirm with “Erase local progress” to restore the starting game state and preferences and cancel reminders. “Cancel” dismisses the confirmation.

When enabled, daily reminders are scheduled for 6:00 and 17:00 local time with “The lake is waking up” and “Your daily bite window is open. Take a quiet cast.” The monthly reminder is scheduled for 10:00 local time on the first day of the month with “A new season on the water” and “Fresh collection goals are ready. Earn keepsake badges at your own pace.”

- “Preview widgets” opens a scrollable preview using current local progress, with the system back control returning to “Settings.” The preview cards have no install or tap action.
- The small Home Screen preview shows “Big Catch,” “Lively water” or “Gentle water,” and “Daily bite window” with the hours.
- The medium Home Screen preview shows “Big Catch,” “Your next lake” or “All lakes discovered,” the lake name, and XP progress. The large preview adds “Trophy room,” species and lake totals, up to three species ranked by best catch weight, and “Season on the water” with progress toward “Catch twelve fish.” Its empty collection reads “Cast your first line to begin your collection.”
- The rectangular Lock Screen preview shows “Daily bite window” and the hours. Installed Lock Screen widgets also come in an inline format with the hours and a circular format with “6–9” and the full hours in its accessibility label.
- The system widget picker lists “Big Catch” with “Bite hours, lake progress and your trophy room.” Tapping an installed large widget opens “Trophy room.” Tapping the other installed formats opens “Discover the lakes.”

## Features

- “Fish” supports casting, cancelling a waiting cast, hooking, managing “Line tension,” and automatically releasing each landed fish after recording it.
- “Your collection” tracks twelve species, best weights, catch counts, and “Recent catches.”
- “Season on the water” tracks monthly catch, species and weight goals and awards keepsake badges.
- “Your tackle” offers three “Rods” and three “Lures” through earned coins. Better rods make tension easier to manage, and upgraded lures improve rare-species odds.
- “Discover the lakes” unlocks four lakes through fishing and idle “Exploration” XP. Each lake has three species.
- “Daily bite window” shows device-local game hours. The device clock also affects species odds. Fishing is available outside those windows.
- “Your waterside supplies” grants 2 coins and 1 exploration XP per completed five-minute interval, for up to eight hours at a time. Landing a fish earns 12 XP and coins based on its weight.
- “Metric weights,” “Gentle haptics,” and “Calm water” customize play. The lake animation also respects the device’s Reduce Motion setting.
- “Local reminders” offers optional daily and monthly notifications. “Widgets” offers offline Home Screen and Lock Screen views with shortcuts into the game.
- “Erase local progress” restores a fresh game through an explicit confirmation.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- **Waiting for the hook.** “Waiting for a ripple” lasts about three to six seconds. Wait for “A bite!” and then tap “Set the hook” within about ten seconds. “Bring the line in” cancels while waiting. If the bite expires, “The fish swam away. Cast again.” appears. Tap “Cast again.”
- **Reeling can stall or fail.** The catch progress bar advances only when tension is strictly between 22% and 82%. Alternate “Reel gently” and “Ease” to stay in that range. A tap on “Reel gently” is a short burst. Very high tension, sustained slack, or taking too long can produce “The line went loose or too tight. Try a gentler rhythm.” Tap “Cast again” to retry. “Calm water” makes the progress bar fill faster within the target range.
- **Lake selection during a cast.** “Fish this lake” and “Fishing here” return to “Fish” even during reeling. Finish the attempt and select the lake again to switch. Leaving the app or interrupting its active state cancels waiting, hooking, or reeling.
- **Gear and lake gates.** “Equipped” cannot be tapped. “120 ◉” and “280 ◉” are disabled until the balance covers the price. Earn coins by landing fish or through “Your waterside supplies.” On locked lakes, “Exploration” replaces the fishing button until the XP threshold is met.
- **Empty collection and rolling history.** “Not discovered yet” is the starting state of every species. Land a fish to record it and reveal “Recent catches.” That list shows only the ten latest entries, while “Caught” and “Best” retain the species totals and records. The widget’s “Cast your first line to begin your collection.” clears after the first catch.
- **Season totals reset.** “Season on the water” starts new progress each calendar month when the app refreshes the season. “Collected badges” is cumulative. “Record 30 kg this month” still uses kilograms when “Metric weights” is off.
- **Idle supplies can be absent.** “Your waterside supplies” appears only when there are newly credited idle coins. The game credits completed five-minute intervals when opening or returning to the app and stops counting after eight hours. “Dismiss” only hides the notice.
- **Reminder controls depend on system permission.** “Remind me” is disabled while the permission or scheduling request runs. If “Disabled in system settings” appears, use “Open system settings” to allow notifications, then return and turn on “Remind me.” If “Could not schedule reminders. Try again.” appears, try the toggle again.
- **Erasing progress restores starter gear and balance.** The confirmed “Erase local progress” action removes the collection, XP, gear upgrades, coins earned above the starting balance, badges, and changed preferences. The starting 80 coins and the original equipped gear reappear. System notification permission is managed separately in device settings.

## Starter content and resume

A fresh game starts with 80 coins, 0 XP, “Willow Lake” unlocked, “Willow rod” and “Copper spoon” equipped, and no sample catches or earned badges. All twelve species are listed as undiscovered. The other three lakes and four tackle upgrades must be earned.

Landed catches, collection records, recent history, coins, XP, lake selection, owned and equipped tackle, monthly progress, badges, and preferences are saved locally. An unfinished cast cannot be resumed after leaving the active app or closing it. Returning to a new session starts on “Fish,” ready for a new cast.

## Permissions

Optional notification alerts and sounds. The app asks when the player first enables “Remind me” under “Settings” → “Local reminders” and the system has yet to record a choice. The permission dialog uses system-provided wording. There is no app-defined usage-description string. No other permission requests appear in the normal UI.

## Absent

Login and accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are absent. “Erase local progress” is a device-local game reset.

## Data and support

Game progress and preferences stay on the device and are shared with the app’s widgets. The app works offline and schedules reminders on the device. System backups follow the user’s device settings. There is no on-screen “Contact” or “Support” control.

## Scanning and health

None.

## Platform

- Native iPhone and iPad app. Minimum iOS and iPadOS version is 17.6.
- iPhone supports upright portrait and both landscape orientations. iPad also supports upside-down portrait. At regular width, the fishing scene and controls can sit side by side. Narrow layouts and accessibility text sizes stack them.
- The app has no region selector or region-based feature gate. Bite windows, catch odds, and reminder schedules follow the device’s local clock. Season rollover follows the device calendar. Times, dates, and numbers use device formatting. Weight units follow “Metric weights.”
- App copy is localized in English, German, French, and Brazilian Portuguese. The display name “Big Catch” is the same in all four. The quoted app copy above is English.
