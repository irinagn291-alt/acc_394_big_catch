Cast a line at Willow Lake in Big Catch, an offline, single-player fishing game for iPhone and iPad. Watch for a bite, set the hook and balance line tension as you reel. Each landed fish is weighed, recorded and released.

Discover 12 species across four illustrated lakes. Earn experience to unlock new waters and spend in-game coins on rods that make tension rise more gently and lures that improve your chances of hooking rarer fish.

Visit the Trophy Room to see personal bests, catch totals and recent catches. Earn keepsake badges by meeting monthly goals for catches, species and total weight.

Choose metric or imperial weights, adjust haptics or switch on Calm water for less scenery animation and faster reeling progress. Optional local reminders announce bite hours and the start of a new month. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show bite hours, with progress and collection details on larger Home Screen widgets.
